About Lumen

Dermatology built around you, not the clock.

Lumen started with a simple complaint we kept hearing: seven-minute visits, rushed explanations, and treatment plans nobody had time to actually understand. We built the opposite.

Our story

Founded on one idea: explain everything

Lumen Dermatology opened in 2008 with two exam rooms and a conviction that patients do better when they understand why a treatment works, not just that it does. Eighteen years later, we're a six-specialist practice — but every visit still ends with a plan you could explain back to a friend.

We keep our own pathology lab on-site, cap how many patients each doctor sees per day, and quote cosmetic work in writing before you commit to anything. None of that is standard in dermatology. We think it should be.
