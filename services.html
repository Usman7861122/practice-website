Before you book

Questions we hear often

Do you accept insurance?

Yes, we're in-network with most major PPO plans for medical visits. Cosmetic and elective procedures are self-pay, with pricing shared in writing before your appointment.

How soon can I be seen?

Most new patients are offered an appointment within the same week. Urgent concerns, like a changing mole, are prioritized for the next available slot.

What should I bring to my first visit?

A photo ID, insurance card if applicable, a list of current medications, and, if you have one, a note of when a concern started and what's changed.

Do you see children?

Yes. Our pediatric dermatology visits are scheduled with extra time and a specialist experienced in treating younger patients comfortably.

Not sure which service fits?

Tell us what's going on and we'll point you to the right specialist.
