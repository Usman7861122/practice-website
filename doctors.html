Our Doctors

Six specialists. One standard of care.

Every physician at Lumen is board-certified through the American Board of Dermatology and sees patients directly — no hand-offs between the consult and the treatment.

AR

Dr. Aisha Rahman

Founder & Medical Dermatology

MD, FAAD — American Board of Dermatology, 19 years in practice.

Founded Lumen in 2008. Focuses on complex acne, eczema, and autoimmune skin conditions.

NO

Dr. Naledi Osei

Cosmetic Dermatology

MD, FAAD — American Board of Dermatology, 11 years in practice.

Known for a conservative, natural-first approach to injectables and skin-quality treatments.

MF

Dr. Marcus Feld

Dermatopathology & Skin Cancer Screening

MD, PhD — American Board of Dermatology, 15 years in practice.

Runs our in-house pathology lab, reading Lumen's own biopsies to shorten result times.

EV

Dr. Elena Vasquez

Surgical Dermatology

MD, FAAD, FACMS — Mohs surgery fellowship, 13 years in practice.

Specializes in skin cancer excision and scar-minimizing surgical technique.

PN

Dr. Priya Nair

Pediatric Dermatology

MD, FAAD — Pediatric dermatology fellowship, 9 years in practice.

Sees patients from infancy through the teen years, with visits paced for younger patients.

SO

Sam Okafor

Laser & Light Therapy

PA-C — Board-certified physician assistant, laser specialist, 8 years in practice.

Leads our laser and light-based treatments, working under Dr. Rahman's supervision.
